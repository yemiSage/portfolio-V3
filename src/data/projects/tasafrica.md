# TASAfrica Case Study - Sports Talent Discovery Platform

## Project Overview
TASAfrica is a professional sports social network designed and engineered to solve visibility, validation, and connectivity challenges for athletes across Africa. It helps young, aspiring talents build professional, verified athletic resumes and connect with international scouts, coaches, academies, and clubs.

- **Role**: Lead Product Designer (UX, UI design, research, prototyping, and design system architecture).
- **Date**: March 2024
- **Industry**: Sports, Talent Acquisition, Social Networking
- **Services Provided**: User Research, Mobile and Web UI Design, Custom Design System Architecture, Wireframing, and Prototyping.
- **Client**: TASAfrica Sports
- **Live Site**: app.tasafrica.com

---

## The Problem Statement
In the African sports landscape, thousands of talented youth athletes are invisible to global recruiters. Conversely, international scouts struggle with:
1. Identifying verified talent.
2. Assessing athletes remotely due to lack of standard performance metrics and high-quality, authentic highlight video clips.
3. Overcoming fragmented communication over insecure platforms.

Before Yemi joined, the early platform treated all users (athletes, scouts, coaches, academies) identically. It lacked structure, creating major drop-offs during onboarding and leaving recruiters overwhelmed by unstructured noise rather than clean, searchable profiles.

---

## User-Centered Goals
The redesigned product had to deliver:
1. **Lightweight & Inclusive Onboarding**: Low cognitive load for users with varied digital literacy and entry-level mobile devices.
2. **Scout-Centric Discoverability**: Clear search and filtering parameters instead of infinite scrolling of generic feeds.
3. **Multi-Role Profile System**: Adaptive user interfaces tailored to each role's specific needs (e.g., performance stats for athletes vs. tactical tracking dashboard for scouts).
4. **Interactive Highlight Clips**: Video-centric modules with performance overlay metrics to make assessing skills immediate and authentic.
5. **Robust Design System**: A scalable, component-based layout framework built in Figma to allow engineers to iterate easily on future product cycles.

---

## Research & Design Process
Yemi and the Product Manager conducted comprehensive qualitative research to ground design choices:
- **Scope**: Interviewed **34+ target users** (including active teenage athletes, international soccer scouts, local club coaches, and academic directors).
- **Insights Gathered**: Scouts do not trust generic video reels; they look for structured match context, official verification checkmarks, and tactical performance breakdowns. Athletes are easily overwhelmed by long, complex sign-up forms.
- **Iterations**: Rejected early desktop-first concepts in favor of a strictly **mobile-first** experience, optimizing for the typical devices and network conditions of the target region.

---

## Key Product Features & User Flows

### 1. Light and Friction-Free Onboarding
- **Flow**: Role Selection -> Essential Credentials -> Instant Identity Verification via OTP -> Account Created.
- **Why it works**: Users enter the product first to experience immediate value, completing more granular profile details (like historical tournaments, height, weight, and skills) in modular steps later.

### 2. Discovery Homepage
- **Flow**: Houses talent category carousels, scout recommendations, and the general community feed.
- **Why it works**: Puts content and talent discovery as the absolute centerpiece of the user experience.

### 3. Community Highlight Feed
- **Flow**: Interactive video-oriented feed allowing athletes to share short video highlights of match moments, and enabling scouts, coaches, and recruiters to react, comment, save, and message.

### 4. Role-Based Profiles (690+ Screens total)
- **Athletes**: Structured panels focusing on video highlights, physical dimensions (height, weight, footedness), match history, and verification badges.
- **Scouts**: Tools to review athlete profiles, direct-message candidates, save prospects, and coordinate recruitment workflows.

---

## Core Design Decisions
1. **Mobile-First Layout**: Every component is optimized for thumb-reachability and small screens, keeping text legibility high.
2. **Unified Design System**: Built a modular, reusable component library to ensure visual and functional consistency while speeding up engineering handoff.
3. **Verification Badging**: Placed clear checkmark indicators on profile headers to symbolize background verified registration, building immediate trust with global recruiters.

---

## Results & Impact
- Shipped a beautifully cohesive **690+ screen design** covering every edge case across the ecosystem.
- Simplified registration, significantly reducing onboarding drop-offs.
- Empowered scouts with the exact physical metrics and video contexts needed to initiate direct trials and contract conversations.
- Connected multiple diverse target audiences into one sleek, understandable, and high-performance product.
