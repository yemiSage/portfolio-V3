# StayAfrika — from product flows to a live platform

StayAfrika is an original product design, not a redesign. Yemi led the design thinking, user flows and UI/UX from initial flows to launch, working with 4 engineers (2 frontend and 2 backend) and a product manager. Design began in December 2025 and the product launched around August 2026 after beta testing.

The case study is available at /projects/stayafrika.

## Ownership and shipped experience

- White-label setup: business logo, favicon, hero content, primary colour and a unique StayAfrika subdomain, with a preview alongside the controls.
- Homefind: property payment plans and inspection scheduling connected to transaction progress in messages.
- Custom guest messaging: reusable templates, guest/trip/listing details, listing selection and scheduling in the listing's time zone.
- Shared listing patterns for Homes, Moments and Essentials.
- CSV import for hosts' existing guests, using first name, last name and email.

## From product flows to a live platform

Within around 2 months of launch, StayAfrika had 36 businesses, including 22 active within the past month. These adoption figures describe business participation; they do not measure booking conversion or isolate the impact of an individual feature. Ownership, design decisions and the shipped experience lead the case study, with adoption figures in a supporting paragraph.
